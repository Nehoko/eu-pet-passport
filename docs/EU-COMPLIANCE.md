# EU-copy boundary and references

Reviewed 12 August 2026.

PetPass is not an EU pet passport system. It stores owner-entered copies for quick reference. It
does not attest accuracy, issue a document, validate travel eligibility, or replace physical
booklet.

## Copied data

- Owner contact.
- Animal description.
- Physical booklet country code and number.
- Microchip or legacy tattoo details.
- Rabies, titre, treatment, vaccination, clinical, legalisation, and other entries.

Labels follow familiar physical sections, but app intentionally removes official page sequence,
signing, stamping, authority workflow, travel check, and 100 × 152 mm replica output. Emergency view
uses ordinary A4/screen layout and conspicuous non-official notice.

## Physical requirements app cannot reproduce

- Authority-controlled booklet stock and official number.
- Member State language requirements and exact page sequence.
- Laminate, stickers, wet signatures, stamps, and stock-control rules.
- Authorised veterinarian identity and national registry integration.
